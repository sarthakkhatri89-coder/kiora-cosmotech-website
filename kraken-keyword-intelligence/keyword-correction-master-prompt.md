# Master Prompt - Keyword Strategy Correction

## Role

Act as a senior SEO strategist, content architect, technical SEO implementer, UX-preserving developer, and conversion-focused website editor working on the Kiora CosmoTech website.

## Objective

Improve the website's keyword relevance, keyword freshness, search intent alignment, topical authority, internal linking, and AI-search readiness without redesigning or damaging the existing website.

## Website Context

Kiora CosmoTech is an India-focused B2B skincare, cosmetic, haircare, derma-care, and personal-care manufacturing website. The business model centers on private label manufacturing, third-party manufacturing, contract manufacturing, custom formulation support, and category-specific product manufacturing pages such as face wash, face serum, sunscreen, moisturizer, shampoo, and hair serum.

The website is not a retail e-commerce store. It is a lead-generation and trust-building website for founders, D2C brands, clinics, salons, distributors, marketing companies, and buyers exploring cosmetic manufacturing in India.

## Source of Truth

Use the keyword intelligence findings below. Do not invent facts, certifications, rankings, customer claims, pricing promises, or production claims not already supported by visible content.

## Audit Summary

### Highest-Priority Findings

1. Private label and skincare hub pages need cleaner keyword ownership.
2. Third-party and contract manufacturing pages overlap too much semantically.
3. Hair serum keyword positioning is stronger on the custom live page than in shared product data.
4. Packaging, MOQ, cost, and quote-readiness clusters need stronger commercial keyword mapping.
5. Several blog guides should support one primary commercial page more explicitly to reduce keyword overlap.

## Non-Negotiable Integrity Rules

- Do not redesign the website.
- Do not change the brand identity unless explicitly requested.
- Do not break existing routes.
- Do not remove working components.
- Do not alter business facts.
- Do not invent certifications, claims, addresses, clients, awards, prices, rankings, or guarantees.
- Do not stuff keywords.
- Do not create hidden SEO text.
- Do not create doorway pages.
- Do not duplicate pages unnecessarily.
- Do not remove working CTAs unless replacing them with stronger versions.
- Preserve responsive behavior.
- Preserve the existing styling system.
- Preserve analytics and tracking code.
- Preserve forms and lead flows.
- Preserve legal and compliance pages.
- Keep copy natural, useful, and people-first.
- Use keywords naturally and contextually.

## Page-Wise Keyword Correction Plan

### Homepage

- Keep primary keyword ownership at:
  - `skincare & cosmetic manufacturer in India`
- Keep the homepage as the umbrella commercial hub.
- Do not overload the hero or first fold with too many product exact-match terms.
- Strengthen internal links to:
  - `/private-label-cosmetics-manufacturer-india`
  - `/third-party-cosmetic-manufacturing-india`
  - `/skincare-manufacturer-india`
  - `/request-quote`

### Private Label Page

- Primary keyword:
  - `private label cosmetics manufacturer in India`
- Secondary keywords:
  - private label skincare manufacturer
  - startup skincare brand manufacturing
  - cosmetic packaging and artwork planning
- Emphasize:
  - launch-ready manufacturing
  - brand customization
  - packaging and artwork discussion
  - first-product planning
- Do not let this page become a duplicate general skincare hub.

### Skincare Manufacturer Page

- Primary keyword:
  - `skincare manufacturer in India`
- Secondary keywords:
  - private label skincare manufacturing
  - skincare product categories
  - face wash, serum, sunscreen, moisturizer manufacturing
- Emphasize:
  - product category architecture
  - range planning
  - routes into specific product pages
- Reduce repetition of purely private-label-launch language that belongs more strongly on the private label page.

### Third Party Page

- Primary keyword:
  - `third party cosmetic manufacturing in India`
- Secondary keywords:
  - production support
  - batch manufacturing
  - manufacturing partner
  - operational execution
- Differentiate from contract page by focusing on:
  - manufacturing handling
  - batch planning
  - documentation flow
  - dispatch and supply support

### Contract Manufacturing Page

- Primary keyword:
  - `contract cosmetic manufacturer in India`
- Differentiate clearly from third-party page.
- Use language around:
  - structured manufacturing model
  - custom operations scope
  - procurement and execution coordination
- Avoid simply repeating “third party cosmetic manufacturing” in key headings unless used in a comparison context.

### Packaging Page

- Primary keyword:
  - `cosmetic packaging options`
- Add or strengthen sections for:
  - face wash packaging
  - face serum packaging
  - sunscreen packaging
  - moisturizer and cream packaging
  - shampoo and body wash packaging
- Link naturally to relevant product pages.

### Request Quote Page

- Primary keyword:
  - `request cosmetic manufacturing quote`
- Add a concise checklist near the top covering:
  - product category
  - pack type
  - MOQ
  - target price band
  - launch timeline
  - service model
- Keep form flow and design intact.

### Hair Serum Page and Shared Product Data

- Treat `app/hair-serum-manufacturer/page.tsx` as the stronger keyword model.
- Update `data/products.ts` references for hair serum so the supporting text aligns with:
  - scalp serum
  - hair growth support serum
  - peptide hair serum
  - density / root / anti-breakage positioning
- Remove or reduce generic finishing-only language where it contradicts the custom live route.

## Metadata Updates

- Preserve canonical URLs.
- Keep titles concise and front-loaded with primary commercial keywords.
- Where metadata is updated:
  - do not overstuff modifiers
  - ensure each page owns one primary term
  - use secondary modifiers only when the page visibly supports them

## Heading and Content Updates

- Keep one clear H1 per page.
- Use H2s to clarify intent boundaries between overlapping pages.
- Add buyer-process language where useful:
  - sampling
  - packaging planning
  - artwork approval
  - MOQ discussion
  - launch planning
  - product category selection

## Internal Linking Updates

- Add or standardize anchors such as:
  - `private label cosmetic manufacturing`
  - `third party cosmetic manufacturing`
  - `skincare manufacturer in India`
  - `face wash manufacturing`
  - `face serum manufacturing`
  - `sunscreen manufacturing`
  - `request cosmetic manufacturing quote`
- Ensure blog posts link to one primary commercial page and not to too many competing destinations in the same paragraph.

## New Pages or Sections to Create

Create only if necessary and only if they fit the current architecture:

- stronger quote-readiness section on quote page
- stronger packaging-by-category sections on packaging page
- clearer manufacturing timeline answer block on manufacturing process or quote page

Do not add new pages casually if a section improvement on an existing page will solve the keyword gap.

## Schema and AI-Search Alignment

- Keep schema consistent with visible page intent.
- Commercial product-manufacturing pages should continue to use service-style semantics, not retail product claims.
- Support AI-search readiness by adding concise direct-answer blocks where the content already justifies them.

## Image Alt Text Instructions

- Keep alt text natural and descriptive.
- Use product / packaging / manufacturing descriptors only when they match the image and visible page context.
- Do not stuff exact-match keywords into alt text.

## FAQ / Direct Answer Block Instructions

- Add or refine FAQs for:
  - packaging options
  - MOQ planning
  - quote requirements
  - service-model differences
  - production timelines
- Keep answers concise, factual, and consistent with visible page content.

## CTA Preservation Instructions

- Preserve existing lead-generation CTAs.
- Improve only the surrounding support copy if needed.
- Do not weaken quote or WhatsApp visibility.

## Mobile and Performance Rules

- Preserve current responsive behavior.
- Do not add heavy libraries or visual experiments.
- Do not introduce hidden accordions that bury critical commercial copy above the fold.
