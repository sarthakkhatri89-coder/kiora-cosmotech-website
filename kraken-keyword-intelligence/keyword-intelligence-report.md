## Keyword Placement Audit

### Strong Placement Patterns

- Titles generally place the commercial keyword at the start.
- H1s are direct and crawlable.
- Hero intros often repeat the core entity and product/service type naturally.
- FAQ sections bring in long-tail terms without looking spammy.
- Internal links already point into quote, service, and related product pages.

### Placement Fixes to Prioritize

1. Standardize buyer-intent anchors into core service pages:
   - `private label cosmetic manufacturing`
   - `third party cosmetic manufacturing`
   - `skincare manufacturer in India`
   - `request cosmetic manufacturing quote`
2. On overlapping service pages, make section H2s more intent-specific:
   - private label page: launch, branding, packaging, artwork, catalog selection
   - third-party page: production partnership, batch execution, manufacturing support, supply continuity
   - contract page: customized procurement and operations model, not a synonym page
3. Align supporting blogs so each one clearly supports a commercial page instead of loosely targeting the same money term.

## Final Assessment

Kiora CosmoTech already has a solid commercial keyword base and a stronger-than-average content structure for a manufacturing-led B2B website. The main work now is not “add more keywords.” It is **tightening ownership**:

- one page per primary commercial term
- one clear purpose per service hub
- one deliberate support role per guide article
- one synchronized source-of-truth narrative for overlapping product routes

That is what will move the site from “good keyword foundation” to “deliberately architected ranking system.”
