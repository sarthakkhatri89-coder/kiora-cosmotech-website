# Content Writer Brief

Project: Kiora CosmoTech  
Objective: strengthen commercial clarity, trust, and topical authority without adding fake proof or risky claims.

## Brand and compliance guardrails

- Use cosmetic-safe language.
- Do not claim cures, guaranteed outcomes, certifications, awards, approvals, or rankings unless they are verified and already supported by the business.
- Keep tone practical, premium, B2B, founder-friendly, and manufacturing-aware.
- Speak like a knowledgeable manufacturing consultant, not a retail beauty brand.

## Priority content tasks

### 1. Strengthen core trust copy around enquiries

Affected areas:

- `/request-quote`
- CTA areas on core commercial pages

Write/support copy for:

- what happens after an enquiry
- what details help the team respond faster
- realistic response expectation
- reassurance without fake promises

Avoid:

- “instant quote”
- “guaranteed launch”
- “best manufacturer”

### 2. Improve city-page trust positioning

Affected areas:

- `/city/*`

Content goal:

- Make city pages clearly about service coverage and buyer intent, not implied local office presence.

Useful content angles:

- “brands from [city] typically discuss…”
- “common product categories for [city] buyers…”
- “how pan-India manufacturing support works…”

Avoid:

- implying a branch office unless true
- using local-office language without proof

### 3. Improve commercial hub share-message alignment

Affected areas:

- About page
- Private Label page
- Third Party page
- Skincare Manufacturer page
- Blog listing

Need:

- tighter meta-supporting copy blocks that express what the page is for in one direct paragraph
- short, specific social-share-compatible summary lines

### 4. Build stronger internal-link context from blog to money pages

Affected areas:

- blog intros, middle CTAs, final CTAs

Need:

- more natural transitions from educational content into:
  - `/private-label-cosmetics-manufacturer-india`
  - `/third-party-cosmetic-manufacturing-india`
  - `/skincare-manufacturer-india`
  - `/request-quote`
  - product manufacturing pages where relevant

Avoid:

- link stuffing
- repeating the exact same anchor in every article

## Content opportunities by page type

### Homepage

- Keep the current direct-answer model.
- Add one more short proof-oriented block only if backed by real process facts:
  - sample approval flow
  - pack-format discussion
  - batch-wise planning
  - pan-India dispatch support

### Service pages

Focus on:

- who the service is for
- when to choose that route
- what inputs buyers should prepare
- what mistakes delay manufacturing

### Product manufacturing pages

Focus on:

- buyer intent
- packaging planning
- formula direction
- claim planning
- launch sequence

### Blog

Target themes for future content:

- packaging compatibility
- artwork mistakes
- product brief templates
- city-wise buyer behavior
- launch sequencing
- documentation-aware claim planning

## Tone examples

Preferred:

- “A useful starting point for brands that need packaging clarity before sampling.”
- “This route is usually better when the founder wants a faster first launch.”
- “Claim language should stay aligned with cosmetic positioning and applicable regulations.”

Avoid:

- “No.1 manufacturer”
- “best quality guaranteed”
- “clinically proven”
- “FDA approved”
- “100 percent results”

## Acceptance checklist

- Copy is human and practical.
- No fake trust signals.
- No medical language.
- Internal links are helpful and natural.
- Page summaries are clearer after the update than before.
