KD-01: Quote form is UI-only

The quote form currently prevents submission and shows a success message without sending data. This is the biggest business risk on the site.

  • Source: components/QuoteForm.tsx
  • Owner: Developer + Growth/CRM
High

KD-02: Schema pattern diverges across commercial pages

Shared pages use a cleaner schema stack than custom Face Wash, Face Serum, Hair Serum, and Sunscreen pages. Governance will drift unless these are normalized.

  • Affected: custom product-manufacturer routes
  • Source: shared renderers + standalone product pages
  • Owner: Developer + SEO
High

KD-03: City pages overstate locality via LocalBusiness schema

City landing pages are service-coverage pages, but schema currently models them as local businesses without city-specific office evidence.

  • Affected: /city/*
  • Source: app/city/[slug]/page.tsx, lib/schema.ts
  • Owner: SEO + Developer
High

KD-04: No analytics or conversion tracking stack

There is no evidence of GA4, GTM, Clarity, Plausible, Umami, or server-side lead event tracking in the current codebase.

  • Affected: site-wide
  • Source: repo-wide source scan
  • Owner: Growth + Developer
