Quote form does not capture leads
Critical Confidence: High Priority: P1
Live traffic can convert visually, but the current form does not send submissions anywhere.
AffectedURL: /request-quote
File: components/QuoteForm.tsx
EvidencehandleSubmit only calls event.preventDefault() and setSubmitted(true). No network request, email integration, server action, webhook, CRM, or thank-you route exists.
Why it mattersThis is the highest-impact commercial failure on the site. SEO can generate traffic, but the primary lead form is currently a UI illusion.
Exact fixReplace the local-only submit handler with a real submission path: server action, API route, CRM webhook, email delivery, or managed form service. Add success + failure states and event tracking.
OwnerDeveloper
EffortMedium
TestingSubmit a test enquiry, verify delivery in the target system, verify error handling, and confirm event tracking fires.

Schema coverage is inconsistent between custom and shared commercial pages
High Confidence: High Priority: P2
Some product pages get richer `Manufacturer` and `WebPage` semantics, while templated product/service pages do not.
AffectedFiles: components/ProductPageRenderer.tsx, components/ContentPageRenderer.tsx
URLs likely affected: /moisturizer-manufacturer, /cream-manufacturer, /shampoo-manufacturer, and service pages rendered by the shared template.
EvidenceThe shared renderer outputs only BreadcrumbList + Service + FAQ, while custom pages such as face wash and sunscreen emit richer page-specific schema.
Why it mattersCommercial pages should have consistent entity clarity. Uneven schema weakens large-scale semantic coverage across the site.
Exact fixExtend shared renderers to inject manufacturerSchema() and a page-level WebPage object, while keeping visible content alignment intact.
OwnerDeveloper
EffortLow-Medium
TestingValidate live JSON-LD types on at least five shared-renderer commercial pages before and after the change.
